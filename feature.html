Our Features

Everything farmers need in one smart platform

Farmer AI Assistant brings important farming tools together in one place to help farmers make better decisions, save time, reduce losses, and improve crop productivity with easy digital support.

🌦

Weather Forecast

Get live weather updates, rainfall prediction, temperature, wind, and humidity details to plan farm work better.

🌱

Crop Recommendation

Find the best crop based on your soil, climate, district, and season so you can improve yield and planning.

🦠

Disease Detection

Upload crop images and detect possible diseases early using AI-based analysis and treatment guidance.

💊

Fertilizer Guide

Get fertilizer suggestions based on crop type and soil condition to support healthier plant growth.

🤖

AI Assistant

Ask farming-related questions and get instant answers in simple language for daily farming support.

📈

Market Prices

Check daily market rates and understand the best time to sell crops for better profit planning.

📍

Location Based Help

Get district and taluka-based guidance that fits your local area and farming conditions.

🧾

Smart Reports

View clean summaries and useful suggestions so farming information stays organized and easy to read.

Benefits

Farmer AI Assistant helps farmers save time, reduce confusion, and make better decisions using one simple platform. It improves crop planning, supports early disease detection, provides weather-based guidance, and helps farmers check market prices before selling crops.

Why It Matters

This project is useful because it brings important farming information together in one place. It helps farmers work more efficiently, reduce losses, and improve productivity with the support of AI.
